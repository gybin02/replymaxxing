ReplyMaxxing v1.0

Drafts LinkedIn openers, replies and comments in your voice and coaches every chat toward a booked call. You always press Send.

Before publishing: set API_BASE in config.js to your server URL (see ../server/DEPLOY.md).

Try it unpacked: chrome://extensions > Developer mode > Load unpacked > pick this folder.
Click the ✦ icon in the toolbar to open the side panel, sign in with your email, pick a plan, answer the 3 minute setup.
